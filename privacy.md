# J.A.R.V.I.S. Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 8, 2026**

## Overview

J.A.R.V.I.S. Personal is a locally operated Windows AI assistant developed for private, personal use.

This policy explains how its optional Google Gmail and Google Calendar integration handles information.

## Information Accessed

With the user's explicit authorization, J.A.R.V.I.S. may access:

- **Gmail:** Message metadata, including sender, subject, date, and labels, to identify and summarize unread messages. The integration does not request message bodies.
- **Google Calendar:** Calendar event information, including titles, dates, and times, to report upcoming appointments.

Access is limited to the read-only permissions granted by the user.

## How Information Is Used

Google information is used to provide personalized briefings and respond to the user's requests.

The Google integration does not send emails, delete messages, modify calendar events, or change message read status.

## Storage and Security

J.A.R.V.I.S. processes Google information locally on the user's Windows computer.

Google authorization tokens are stored locally using Windows encryption. Credentials and account information are not published on this website.

The integration does not intentionally save retrieved email messages or calendar event details to permanent files.

## Data Sharing

The Google integration communicates with Google's APIs to retrieve authorized information.

It does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transmit it to third parties for marketing.

The public J.A.R.V.I.S. website does not collect or store Gmail messages, calendar events, or Google authorization tokens.

## User Control and Revocation

Users can revoke J.A.R.V.I.S.'s Google access through their Google Account's third-party connection settings.

Locally stored authorization credentials can also be deleted from the user's computer to disconnect the integration.

## Availability

J.A.R.V.I.S. is currently a personal-use project and is not offered as a public service.

## Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the project owner through the associated GitHub account.
